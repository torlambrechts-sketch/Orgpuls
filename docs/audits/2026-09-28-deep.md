## Findings

Most severe first. "Found by" names the auditor that reproduced it; "verified" means the lead auditor re-ran it.

### P0

**AUD-01 · P0 · Any member can read who has not answered.**
*Promise:* `malinger.privacy` «Systemet vet hvem som ikke har svart … Ingen i virksomheten kan se den lista — verken du, lederne eller verneombudet»; `arshjulet.step.remind.note`; the anonymity article.
*Evidence (verified):* policy `outbox_read` = `app.is_org_member(org_id)` and `grant select on app.outbox to authenticated` (0020, kept by 0026) — same on hosted (read-only catalogue query; 112 reminder rows there). Reminders are queued only for `responded_at is null` (0076, 0096). As the fixture's daglig leder: `select count(*), count(distinct e.full_name) from app.outbox o join app.employees e on e.id = o.employee_id where o.kind in ('paminnelse','siste_paminnelse')` → **34 | 34**. `last_error = 'answered_or_expired'` also marks who answered between queueing and sending. Verneombud and avdelingsleder have the same policy.
*Fix:* revoke client SELECT on `app.outbox`; the one app reader (`lib/wheel/read.ts:129`) only counts rows — serve the count from a definer RPC that returns no employee.
*Test to add:* a suite row: as each member role, `select` on `app.outbox` is denied; the count RPC returns a number and no id.

**AUD-02 · P0 · `public.conversations` releases each commenter's answer and ignores complementary suppression.**
*Evidence (probe, `probes/kprobe.sql`, `kprobe2.sql`):* it gates on "the group has ≥ k responses", not on `app.group_release`/`cell_release`, and returns `answer_value` with every comment (`lib/conversations/read.ts:119` passes it to the screens). Group case: group B (5, `protected`) returns five answer values; with the released whole-organisation statement index, (9×56 − 5×100)/4 recovers group A's (4, `insufficient_data`) mean exactly. Minimal case: 5 respondents, 4 comment → `5×80 − Σ` gives the fifth person's answer. `comment_themes` refuses the same data (it gates on k people). Code from 0018/0022; D-155's drill-down panel is a new place that shows it.
*Fix:* drop or coarsen `answer_value`, or require the statement's released cell and at least k non-commenters.
*Test to add:* `conversations` for a 5-person round with 4 commenters returns no answer values; with a protected group, no value that combines with the whole to a withheld group.

**AUD-03 · P0 · `public.screening_counts` answers while a round is open.**
*Evidence (verified in code; probe by the promise auditor):* 0023 (never redefined) checks membership and k only — no `status = 'lukket'`, unlike `results_summary`. On the fixture's last round set to `apen`: `{"n":28,"status":"ok","questions":[{"key":"krenkende","options":[{"n":24},{"n":2},…]}]}` while `results_summary` said `not_available`. Polling between submissions tells a watcher how the newest respondent answered on harassment and violence. Every member role can call it. (`conversations` is also not gated on close; whether a live comment thread is intended is a product decision, but with AUD-02 it is not safe.)
*Fix:* return `not_available` until the round is closed.
*Test to add:* `report_invariants` row: `screening_counts` on an open round is `not_available`.

**AUD-04 · P0 (law, § 6-2) · With the Veiviser box unticked, the verneombud is never pre-notified — the employees are.**
*Promise:* `arshjulet.lead` «verneombudet varsles alltid før de ansatte»; `oppsett.roller.can.verneombud` «Varsles først»; `veiviser.safety.duty`.
*Evidence (probe, two `wheel_tick`s):* unticked, `wizard-actions.ts:105,118-123` writes verneombud and tillitsvalgte `lead_days 0`, alle ansatte 1. `wheel_tick` queues the lead-0 notice in the same run that opens the round; `dispatch_claim` then fails it `round_already_open`. Tick 1 sent daglig leder and alle ansatte; tick 2 marked verneombud, tillitsvalgte and avdelingsledere `round_already_open`, never sent.
*Fix:* a lead of 0 means "the same day, before opening" — queue it before step 2 opens the round, or floor the verneombud's lead at 1 and say so in the Veiviser.
*Test to add:* `wheel_invariants`: with a lead-0 verneombud row, the verneombud's `forvarsel` is sent (not `round_already_open`) before any `invitasjon`.

### P1

**AUD-05 · P1 · Årshjulet's lead chip does nothing when the value chosen equals the stored one.** (Code review and promise audit, independently; mine, 0103.) A Veiviser wheel has `notify_lead_days = 14` (column default) and a ladder at 2/2/1, so no chip is lit; choosing 14 writes 14 → 14, the trigger's `when (old … is distinct from new …)` is false, the ladder stays at 2, «Lagret» shows, and a reload un-lights the chip. `evaluation_invariants.sql` row 2 asserts the no-op as intended. *Fix:* let `saveWheel` set the three rungs, or compare the trigger against the verneombud rung. *Test:* ladder 2/2/1 + `notify_lead_days` 14, choose 14 → 14/14/14.

**AUD-06 · P1 · The invitation still says results come «når fristen er ute».** (Code review, promise audit; mine, 0105.) `mail.invitasjon.results` is printed whenever the ladder tells everyone, while 0105 holds the employees' notice and page until `results_publish_on` (default close + 7, up to 60). The daglig leder sees the sentence in the new send preview, under the date field. *Fix:* carry the date in the job and say it, or drop the time from the sentence. *Test:* `mail.test.ts` — an invitation with a publish date after the close does not promise results at the deadline.

**AUD-07 · P1 (latent) · A survey-only language drops out entirely after this release — X-086 says otherwise.** (Code review; verified in `lib/i18n/offered.ts:60`.) pl/uk/lt/sv/da are offered only when *every* source path is approved; 0105 added 11 page paths and 9 mail paths with no approved translation, so a piloting organisation's survey and invitations fall back wholly to bokmål — not "the new strings in bokmål" as X-086 records. Hosted has **no** `locale_pilots` rows and the language flags are off, so no respondent is affected today. *Fix:* tolerate paths added after approval (per-string fallback), or ship translations; correct X-086. *Test:* `offeredLocales` with a registry complete for the old source plus one new path keeps the language.

**AUD-08 · P1 · Avdelingsledere never get the pre-notice in an organisation set up by the Veiviser.** `wizard-actions.ts:121` writes their rung at lead 0; same mechanism as AUD-04. *Test:* as AUD-04 for the avdelingsledere rung.

**AUD-09 · P1 · The drill-down's comment panel states things that are not true for two roles.** (All three reviewers; mine, D-155.) `conversations` refuses the verneombud (`not_available`) and scopes an avdelingsleder to their own groups; `getConversations` turns both into "nothing", so the verneombud reads «Ingen har skrevet en kommentar om dette» over existing comments, and the avdelingsleder «Kommentarene gjelder hele virksomheten» over their own groups' only (probe: 8 threads vs the daglig leder's 18). Nothing leaks; the text is false. *Fix:* carry "not available" apart from "empty"; show `commentsWhole` only for the whole scope. *Test:* Resultater as verneombud and avdelingsleder with fixture comments.

**AUD-10 · P1 · The send card and the thank-you promise sharing that may not happen.** (Code review, promise audit; mine.) `maleoppsett.send.publishNote` «Alle ansatte får dem denne dagen» is shown unconditionally; without the ladder's «alle ansatte» rung nothing is sent, and with `results_page` off the page stays closed (the thank-you checks the rung but not the page). *Fix:* show both only when the rung exists and the page is on (`round_send_preview` already returns `results_shared`).

**AUD-11 · P1 · «startet {dato}» and «Siden sist» dates are not when anything happened.** (Code review, promise audit; mine, 0105.) `pulse_reasons` includes `besluttet` measures and falls back to `created_at`; the step backfill logs an unfinished measure at `updated_at` (any edit); the fixture writes no `measure_steps`, so every QA/demo measure carries the seed day, and a demo copy adds a `now()` row. "Never fabricate data." *Fix:* no "startet" for besluttet; don't treat `updated_at` as a start; emit steps from the fixture; skip the trigger row on demo copy. *Test:* a measure edited after the grunnlinje does not appear as changed since; a besluttet measure has no start date.

**AUD-12 · P1 · A future-dated evaluation silences the § 9-2 reminder.** (All three reviewers; mine, 0103.) No bound on `held_on` (DB or Zod): as verneombud, `held_on = 2099-01-01` → `due_on 2100-01-01`, no reminder, and Rapport prints «Evaluert 1. januar 2099». *Fix:* `check (held_on <= current_date)` in the org's zone + Zod refine. *Test:* `evaluation_invariants` row refusing a future date.

**AUD-13 · P1 · Invariant I3 has had no working proof since the respondent intro page (P1-4).** `qa/e2e/invariants.spec.ts:28` expects «Hopp over» on first load; the survey opens on «Før du starter», so I3 ("the respondent page sends nothing that names the respondent") fails before it checks anything. The spec is not in CI (`npm run test:invariants` is manual), so it went stale unseen. *Fix:* start the flow before asserting; run `@invariants` in CI.
