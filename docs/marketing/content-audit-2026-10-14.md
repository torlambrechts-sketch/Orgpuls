## Reading

Asked by Tor on 2026-10-14 over Sentral › Growth › Keywords: «Evaluate our current articles, platform pages and
external facing pages. How can we optimize for SEO and GEO based on this results?» The table below is the state after
the day's changes (D-282); the numbers in this section are what the audit found before them.

**Where the site stands.** 58 public pages, about 60 000 words, all server-rendered with titles, descriptions,
canonical URLs, Article, FAQPage, Organization and BreadcrumbList data, a sitemap, `/llms.txt`, and nothing closed to
the AI crawlers. Search Console gave the site 120 impressions and no clicks in the 28 days to 2026-10-11, across 18
pages; Sentral recorded the first two Google visits on 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-13, to the anonymity article and the
QPSNordic template.

**What the queries say.** The queries that reach the first page are questions, and each lands on a section that
answers it:

- «hvordan øker man svarprosenten i pulsundersøkelser?» → the pulse article, position 2;
- «hva er den beste plattformen for pulsundersøkelser?» → /plattform, 6;
- «minimum antall svar for anonym rapportering i medarbeiderundersøkelse» → the anonymity article, 6;
- «hvilken plattform tilbyr anonym medarbeiderfeedback?» → the anonymity article, 7;
- «hva betyr motstridende» → the conflicting-demands article, 11.

The head terms sit at 40–80: «verneombud psykososialt arbeidsmiljø» (16 impressions, 60), «medarbeiderundersøkelse
spørsmål» (44), «anonym medarbeiderundersøkelse» (16 on the article, 63 on /smaa-bedrifter). That is the authority
gap the strategy names (§ 1): a three-week-old domain nobody links to yet. The questions are where the site can win
now, because an AI answer and a long-tail search both lift a single section that asks and answers.

**What the pages looked like against that.** Before today:

- 16 of 25 articles had fewer than half their sections phrased as questions; the factor articles were the thinnest
  (kontakt 1 of 10, integritet 1 of 7, emosjon 2 of 10, leder 2 of 10);
- 8 factor articles cited two sources;
- 5 titles ran past 65 characters (handel 70, kunnskap og kontor 68, barnehage og skole 68, its question page 68,
  Bransjer 68);
- 2 H1s did not ask (emosjonelle krav, /verneombud); 1 description passed 155 (/demo);
- the «which tool» page had one inbound link; the article index outranked its own anonymity article for one query.

The product pages (home, Plattform, Bruksområder, Bransjer, Pris) are the design's and are not measured section by
section here; their titles and descriptions are within limits. The templates are short (136–307 words) by design.

**What changed today.** The sections ask: in 24 articles, headings that answered a question now put the question
(«Hva betyr en lav score på …?», «Hvordan måles … i en undersøkelse?», «Hvilke tiltak …?»); the pulse and anonymity
articles answer the two observed questions in their first sentence; the eight factor articles cite STAMI's QPSNordic
guide as a third source; the two H1s ask; the five titles and the description fit; /plattform's description names
«pulsundersøkelser»; the «which tool» page is linked from the five industry pages and /sikkerhet and links /plattform;
the Organization carries its founding date and address. Every flag is held by a test now, and the table below shows
none open.

**What this does not fix.** Authority. Nothing on the site moves «verneombud psykososialt arbeidsmiljø» from 60 to
10; links and mentions from other sites do (strategy § 6). The next reading should be four to six weeks after
2026-10-12, when the D-281 pages are indexed.

**Next, in order.** Run this audit weekly with the Search Console export. Enrich the QPSNordic template page, the
first page Google sent a visitor to. Decide whether the «which tool» page goes into the Bruksområder menu (a design
decision). Run the audit for Empvibe and Sweden once they have four weeks of Search Console data.
